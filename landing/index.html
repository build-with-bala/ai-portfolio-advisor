A forecastis a range.

AI Portfolio Advisor ranks NSE stocks for your risk profile and shows the evidence behind every call. Each one comes with a pessimistic, a median and an optimistic outlook for the next 21 trading days, so you see how unsure the model is.

You state your limits. It returns a ranked book.

Tell it your risk profile, your capital and the limits you will not cross. It scores every stock in its universe on four kinds of evidence and marks each one with one of four calls. Change the profile and the same stocks get different answers.

Four kinds of evidence, each one on show.

A single score hides disagreement. Here the four pillars sit side by side for every stock, so you can see when the chart looks strong but the balance sheet does not.

  • Technical

    What the price has been doing. Momentum, trend and how jumpy the stock is. Strong momentum adds to the score; high volatility takes away from it.

  • Fundamental

    What the business looks like. Valuation, profitability, debt, margins, dividends and growth, each compared with the other stocks in the universe.

  • Model forecast

    What the model expects. Three forecasts for the next 21 trading days: a pessimistic case (P10), a median (P50) and an optimistic case (P90). A wide gap means low confidence.

  • Portfolio fit

    What it does to your book. Recent volatility, worst fall, and how closely the stock moves with what you would already hold.

Then it sizes each position, and shows the cash left over.

Sizing uses Hierarchical Risk Parity. Stocks that move together are grouped, and risk is shared between groups before it is shared within them, so three similar bank stocks do not quietly become half your portfolio.

  1. Group the chosen stocks by how alike their price moves are.
  2. Split risk down the tree, group by group.
  3. Cap any position that exceeds your profile's limit.
  4. Round to whole shares at your capital.
  5. Show the cash that could not be placed, instead of hiding it.

Ask why, and get an answer per stock.

Every stock page lists the inputs that pushed its forecast up or down, and by how much. If a call rests on one noisy signal, you can see that before you act on it.

The method is SHAP, a standard way to attribute a model's output to its inputs.

Tested on data the model never saw.

The model is trained on one stretch of history and judged on a later one. A 10-trading-day gap is cut out between them, and overlapping samples are removed, so nothing from the test period leaks back into training.

The app's Validation page reports the error, how often the direction was right, how well the ranking held, and how often the real outcome landed inside the P10 to P90 range.

A plan for getting in, in stages.

The portfolio page lays out a staged schedule for building the positions instead of one large order. It is a plan for you to read and adapt. The product sends nothing to a broker.

Live NSE prices, kept apart from the forecast.

Connect an Upstox or Zerodha market feed and live quotes appear as an overlay. The daily forecast is not quietly recalculated on every tick. If live price action weakens a BUY to a WATCH, the page tells you the change came from the live layer, and shows the feed's status and last tick.

Under the hood

For the examiners. Built for the course AI and ML for Digital Business Managers as a capstone, in Python end to end.

Streamlit interface, optional FastAPI service, LightGBM, SHAP and scikit-learn. A notebook trains the models and exports one artifact; the app reads it and refuses to produce recommendations if it is missing or malformed.

  • UniverseLiquid NSE India equities, daily OHLCV plus fundamental ratios.
  • FeaturesRSI, MACD histogram, Bollinger bandwidth, ATR, stochastic %K, OBV change, Williams %R, 5-day and 21-day returns, rolling volatility and a fractionally differentiated close, followed by redundancy-aware feature selection.
  • TargetForward return over 21 trading days.
  • ModelsThree LightGBM regressors with quantile (pinball) loss at 0.10, 0.50 and 0.90. The P10 to P90 width is used as the uncertainty measure.
  • ValidationPurged, embargoed time-series split with a 10-day embargo. Reported: MAE, RMSE, directional accuracy, mean information coefficient, quantile coverage and realised return by signal.
  • Decision layerCross-sectional technical and fundamental scores, the forecast, and risk gates on volatility, drawdown and confidence, weighted by profile into four states.
  • AllocationHierarchical Risk Parity on the correlation structure, tilted by composite evidence, capped per position, rounded to whole shares, with explicit cash.
  • ExplainabilityPer-stock SHAP values from the serialized model.
  • Live overlayUpstox Market Data Feed V3 or Zerodha Kite WebSocket adapters, with a labelled delayed fallback and a no-quote default.

What this is not.

It is a research and education tool. Use it to study how evidence, uncertainty and sizing fit together, then make your own decisions.

  • It places no trades. There is no broker connection for orders. Nothing is bought or sold.
  • It is not a registered investment adviser. Nothing here is personal investment advice.
  • It promises no returns. A forecast is an estimate with a range, and the real outcome can fall outside it.
  • A backtest is not a track record. Validation figures describe past data only.
  • It is a capstone, not a production system. Real use would still need transaction costs and taxes, corporate-action handling, paper trading and an independent review.
